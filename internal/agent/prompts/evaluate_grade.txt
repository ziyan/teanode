Answer with one JSON object and nothing else:

{"answerVerdict": "correct", "verdictReason": "one short line"}

answerVerdict is one of:

- `correct`: it says what the correct answer says. Extra true detail is fine; different wording is fine.
- `partial`: some of the correct answer and nothing wrong, with part missing.
- `stale`: it gives the outdated answer as the present one.
- `not_known`: it says it does not know. Right when the correct answer is itself "not known".
- `invented`: it answers when the correct answer is "not known", or states something the correct answer contradicts that is not the outdated answer.
- `wrong`: anything else that is not correct, including an answer that says it does not know when the correct answer is known.

When the correct answer is "not known", `not_known` is the only good verdict.
