## What to answer

Three to eight groups, each a theme a reader would look for by name:
one part of the thing, one period of its life, one kind of concern. Each
group gets a short name, a slug for its path (lower-case words joined by
dashes), and the numbers of the facts that belong in it. A fact that fits
nowhere stays where it is: leave it out. A new group needs at least three
facts, or it is not a page.
