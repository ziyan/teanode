## Worth opening

A screenshot in a thread about something going wrong -- an error, a
crash, a build that failed, a machine that stopped. A terminal, a log, a
dashboard, a configuration, a stack trace. A photograph of equipment, a
site, a whiteboard, a drawing or a printed page. Anything somebody was
asked to look at or explain: "look at this", "here's the error", "what
does this mean", "this is what it does".

The words around a picture are the strongest signal there is. A picture
in a thread about a failure, with a sentence pointing at it, is almost
always worth its cost.

## Not worth opening

An avatar, a profile picture, a logo, a banner, an icon, a signature
image, an emoji, a sticker, a reaction. A meme or a joke picture in a
social channel. A photograph of food, a pet or a holiday. A picture with
nothing said around it in a channel that is not about work.

Anything of only a few kilobytes: it is too small to hold text anybody
could read, so whatever it shows, opening it buys nothing.

When nothing around a picture says anything either way, leave it. There
are far more of these than there is money to open them, one passed over
today can be opened later, and money spent today cannot be got back.
