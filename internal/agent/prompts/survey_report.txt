## What to write

A report in markdown, in {{.KnowledgeLanguage}} unless the question is in
another language, in which case in the question's. Its sections follow
the question: a question asking for strengths, weaknesses and what to
do next gets a section for each; one asking how the parts fit together
gets the parts and then how they fit. Open with two or three sentences
that answer the question outright.

Combine rather than list: where parts say the same thing, say it once;
where they disagree, say so and cite both. Keep every citation a part
gives, as it gives it (`projects/example-app`, `projects/example-app#3`,
a document's path), beside the point it supports. Write nothing the
parts do not say: this is read as what the notes say, and a point
without a part behind it is a guess.

Answer with the report and nothing else.
