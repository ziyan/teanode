A picture was found on a web page for an item on a shopping list. Look at the picture and say whether it shows this item as somebody would buy it: the product in its packaging, or the thing itself for a plain item such as a fruit, a vegetable or a loaf of bread. Answer with one JSON object and nothing else:

{"isMatch": true}

Answer {"isMatch": false} when the picture shows anything else, or when you are not sure: a dish or a recipe made with the item, a different product or a different kind of the thing, a store's shelves or aisle, a logo, a banner or an advertisement for an app, a stock photo of groceries in general, or a picture too small or unclear to tell. A wrong picture is worse than none, so only a clear match is true. When the item names a store's own brand, a picture of another brand's product is false.
