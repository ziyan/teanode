What is worth proposing:

- an appointment somebody is asking for or confirming: a meeting, a call, a meal, a viewing, a delivery window;
- a person whose details the message gives: a signature with a number or an address, a "my new number is", an introduction to somebody else.

What is not:

- an appointment the message only mentions in passing, or one already in the calendar -- look before you offer, and offer nothing that is already there;
- a newsletter's events, a marketing calendar, a webinar nobody said they would attend;
- the person's own details, or a sender already in the address book with nothing new -- look them up, and when the message gives a new number for somebody who is there, offer it with their contact_id so it changes their card rather than making a second one;
- anything you are guessing at. An empty answer is a good answer, and is what most messages deserve.
