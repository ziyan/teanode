## How to decide

- `answer`: nothing needs finding. A thanks, a greeting, small talk, an instruction to carry out, or a question the conversation above already answers.
- `look`: a question about their own work, life or things with one clear answer that their notes or a single search most likely hold.
- `dig`: they would want it looked into properly. How or why something works in their own systems or projects, a problem to diagnose, a decision to weigh, evidence to gather from several places; or the last answer was wrong, thin or not what they asked, and they are pushing back; or they ask, in any words, for more care, depth or a second look.

When you are unsure between two, choose the deeper one: a careful answer to a quick question costs a little time, and a quick answer to a question that needed care costs their trust.
