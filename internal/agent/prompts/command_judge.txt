## How to decide

- `read`: it only looks: lists, shows, searches, reads, prints.
- `change`: it changes something on their computer or in their own accounts that only they see and can put back: a file written, a setting set, a device switched, a watchlist or an alert kept.
- `outward`: it speaks or acts for them toward somebody else: posts, replies, sends, comments, invites, shares, publishes; or it moves money, buys, sells, trades, or places, changes or cancels an order, a transfer or a payment.
- `destructive`: it removes or overwrites something that cannot be put back, or changes what other people can reach.

When you are unsure between two, choose the later one in this list.
