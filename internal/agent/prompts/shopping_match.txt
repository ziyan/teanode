How to tie them:
- A receipt prints names short and in capitals ("4 BEAN CHILI FRZN", "APPLES FUJI"). Tie a line to an item when it is plainly the same thing: the same product, or a kind of what the item asks for ("APPLES FUJI" buys "Apples, Fuji preferred"). A brand or a size the item does not ask about does not stop a tie.
- Each line ties to at most one item, and each item to at most one line. Leave out a line that bought nothing on the lists, and an item no line bought.
- quantity is how many of the item the line bought, when the line says (a count, not a weight); leave it "" when it does not.
- isSure is true when the line can only be that item, and false when it might be something else on the lists or might be another thing altogether. A sure tie checks the item off; an unsure one is shown to {{.PersonName}} to confirm, so say false rather than guess.
- Answer {"shoppingMatches": []} when nothing on the receipt is on the lists.
