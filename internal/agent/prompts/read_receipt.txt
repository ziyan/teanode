Record a purchase or an order {{.PersonName}} made. Most order emails do not say how it was paid, and many never name the card: a receipt that does not say how it was paid is still a purchase, so record it. Answer {"isPurchase": false} and nothing more only when the receipt itself shows it was paid another way, or it is not a purchase at all. Paid another way is cash, a gift card or store credit for the whole amount, points, or paid by somebody else. Not a purchase is a newsletter, an advertisement or an offer; a quote, an estimate or a cart not yet bought; a bill or an invoice still to be paid; a shipping or delivery notice with no amounts; and a refund.

Otherwise answer in this shape, leaving out any field the receipt does not show:

{"isPurchase": true, "merchantName": "…", "merchantReceiptNumber": "…", "purchasedOn": "2026-09-30", "purchasedAt": "2026-09-30T14:05:00-04:00", "currencyCode": "USD", "subtotalAmount": "…", "totalAmount": "…", "paymentAccountMask": "1234", "receiptLines": [{"lineNumber": 1, "receiptLineKind": "item", "description": "…", "quantity": "2.53", "quantityUnit": "lb", "unitPriceAmount": "1.59", "lineAmount": "4.02", "taxClassCode": "t"}]}

How to read it:
- One entry in receiptLines per printed line that is money, in the order printed, exactly as printed. lineNumber is its place among them, from 1.
- receiptLineKind is item, discount, tax, fee or tip. A discount, a promotion or a coupon is a line of its own with a negative lineAmount, and discountedLineNumber is the lineNumber of the item it is printed under, when it is under one. Each tax is a line of its own, with the receipt's mark for it as taxClassCode; give the same mark on the items it covers. Shipping, delivery, handling, a service charge and a bag are each a fee line, wherever the receipt prints them, before the subtotal or after it; a gratuity is a tip.
- Leave out what is not money paid: "You saved" and savings totals, points, the subtotal and total lines themselves (give them as subtotalAmount and totalAmount), the payment line and change.
- Amounts are decimal strings with no thousands separators and no currency sign: 1234.50, not $1,234.50. currencyCode is the three-letter code.
- paymentAccountMask is only the digits of the card or account the receipt says paid, when it shows them; leave it out when it does not.
- purchasedOn is the day of the purchase, or the day of the order for an order email. Give purchasedAt only when a time is printed, with its offset when you can tell it.
- Every line must come to the total, to the cent, and the items and discounts (with the fees, when the receipt prints them before the subtotal) to the subtotal. When they do not, read the receipt again; never change a number to make them add up.
- Never invent a field, a line or a number the receipt does not show.
