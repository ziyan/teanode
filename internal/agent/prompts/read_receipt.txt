How to read it:
- One entry in receiptLines per printed line that is money, in the order printed, exactly as printed. lineNumber is its place among them, from 1.
- receiptLineKind is item, discount, tax, fee or tip. A discount, a promotion or a coupon is a line of its own with a negative lineAmount, and discountedLineNumber is the lineNumber of the item it is printed under, when it is under one. Each tax is a line of its own, with the receipt's mark for it as taxClassCode; give the same mark on the items it covers. A bag, delivery or service charge is a fee; a gratuity is a tip.
- Leave out what is not money paid: "You saved" and savings totals, points, the subtotal and total lines themselves (give them as subtotalAmount and totalAmount), the payment line and change.
- Amounts are decimal strings with no thousands separators and no currency sign: 1234.50, not $1,234.50. currencyCode is the three-letter code.
- paymentAccountMask is only the digits of the card or account the receipt says paid, when it shows them.
- purchasedOn is the day of the purchase, or the day of the order for an order email. Give purchasedAt only when a time is printed, with its offset when you can tell it.
- The items, discounts and fees must come to the subtotal, and the subtotal with the taxes and tips to the total, to the cent. When they do not, read the receipt again; never change a number to make them add up.
- Never invent a field, a line or a number the receipt does not show.
