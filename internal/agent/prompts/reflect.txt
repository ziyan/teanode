{{end}}## What to write

At most {{.Most}} observations, each one of these kinds:

- **pattern**: something that happens again and again.
- **tension**: two things that pull against each other.
- **trend**: something moving one way over time.
- **risk**: something that could go wrong, and what it rests on.
- **question**: something that matters and nothing above answers.

Each is one or two sentences, and cites at least two of the pages or
facts above that it rests on, written exactly as they appear above: a
page by its path (`projects/example-app`), a fact by its path and number
(`projects/example-app#3`). An observation that rests on one thing is
that thing said again, and is not written. Nothing that is not above
may be cited.

Fewer is better than weaker. An observation any page above already
states is not one; neither is a summary of the {{if .IsAcrossThemes}}themes{{else}}theme{{end}}. Where
there is nothing worth saying, answer with an empty list.
