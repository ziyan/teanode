How to read a paystub:
- One entry in paystubLines per printed line with an amount, in the order printed: every earning, every tax and every deduction. Leave out lines of hours only ("Worked Hours", "PTO" with no amount), the total lines themselves (give them as grossAmount, totalTaxAmount, totalDeductionAmount and netAmount), time off balances and the withholding settings.
- An earning with nothing this period but an amount for the year to date (a bonus paid earlier) is still a line: give its yearToDateAmount alone.
- Money paid back that the paystub prints outside gross pay, as a non-taxable earning or a reimbursement (expenses, a stipend paid as a refund), is a line of kind "reimbursement", not an earning. Only taxable pay is an earning, and grossAmount and yearToDateGrossAmount are the earnings alone: when a paystub prints "Total Taxable Earnings" apart from "Non-Taxable Earnings", gross is the taxable total, this period and for the year to date, and never includes a reimbursement's year to date, even when this period's is nothing.
- An amount printed negative (a reversal, a refund, a correction) is given negative.
- paystubTaxKind is federal_income, social_security, medicare, medicare_additional (the additional Medicare tax, when printed apart), state_income, local_income, state_disability (a state's disability or family leave insurance) or other. A state tax names the state's two-letter code as taxJurisdictionCode; a local tax names the locality.
- When the paystub prints the wages each tax was figured on ("Wages This Period", "Wages YTD", "Taxable Wages"), give them as currentWagesAmount and yearToDateWagesAmount.
- paystubDeductionKind is retirement_traditional (a 401(k), 403(b) or 457 before tax), retirement_roth (a Roth contribution), hsa (a health savings account), health_premium (medical, dental or vision), fsa_health, fsa_dependent_care, commuter, insurance (life, disability, AD&D) or other. When the paystub prints the employer's part beside the employee's, give it as employerCurrentAmount and employerYearToDateAmount; a benefit only the employer pays is a deduction line with a currentAmount of 0.00 and the employer's part.
- payDate is the day paid ("Pay Date", "Check Date"), not the end of the period.
- Gross pay less the taxes less the employee's deductions must come to the take-home pay, to the cent. When it does not, read the paystub again; never change a number to make it add up.

How to read a W-2:
- A W-2 PDF repeats the same form several times (Copy B, Copy C, Copy 2): read it once.
- One entry per box that prints something; leave out empty boxes. payerName and payerTaxId are the employer's (boxes c and b), recipientName the employee's (box e).
- boxQualifier tells boxes of one number apart: box 12's code letter (D, W, DD, AA…); box 13's checked boxes only, as statutory_employee, retirement_plan or third_party_sick_pay with isChecked true; box 14's label; the state's two-letter code in boxes 15, 16 and 17, one set per state line; the locality's name in boxes 18, 19 and 20.
- isCorrected is true for a W-2c, and its documentKind is still "w2". A W-2c prints the boxes it corrects in two columns, "previously reported" and "correct information": give only the boxes it corrects, with the correct information, never the previously reported.

For both:
- Amounts are decimal strings with no thousands separators and no currency sign: 1234.50, not $1,234.50.
- Never invent a field, a line, a box or a number the document does not show.
