How to read it:
- One entry per thing to buy, in the order written. itemDescription is what it is, short and in the list's words, without the count ("Apples", "4 Bean Chili", "Yogurt, plain, whole milk").
- neededQuantity is how many, as a number ("2"); "1" when the list gives none. "2 of the 4 Bean Chili" is 2 of "4 Bean Chili": a number that is part of a product's name stays in the name. quantityUnit is a unit when one is written (lb, kg, pack, dozen, bottle), else "".
- itemDetails is everything else written about the item, in the list's own words, joined into one line: preferences, sizes, colors, alternatives, who it is for, doubts ("Fuji preferred, others are fine"; "Color: green; size: large; firm, for slicing"). Keep every preference: it is what the shopper reads in the store.
- aisleName is the section or aisle heading the item is under, written plainly with a capital first letter ("Frozen", "Dairy", "Produce and refrigerated"), or "" when there is none.
- storeName at the top is the store the whole list is for when it names one (a heading such as [Example Market]), else "". An item's own storeName is only for an item written for another store than the list's.
- isStoreBrand is true for a product only that store sells under its own name (a store's own snacks or frozen meals), and false for a plain thing anybody sells (tomatoes, milk, soy sauce).
- Never invent an item or a detail that is not written.
