How to read it:
- institutionName is the bank or card issuer as the statement names it; accountName the account's own name or label (Savings, a card's product name) when one shows.
- accountNumber is only the digits shown, masked ones as they are (****1234), with isAccountNumberPartial true when only some show. Leave it out when no number shows; never put a word in its place.
- statementAccountKind is bank (checking, savings), card (a credit card) or other.
- financeAccountId is the id of one of {{.PersonName}}'s accounts imported from statements, listed below, when the statement is of it: the same bank, the same kind and currency, and its last digits where both show them. A bank goes by several names, an abbreviation, its name in English and its name in the local language, and a card by its issuer's or its product's name in either language: match the bank, not the spelling. When the statement shows no number and one listed account at that bank is of the same kind and currency, it is that account. Leave it out when two could be it, or when it is of none of them.
- syncedFinanceAccountId is the id of one of the accounts synced from banks, listed below, when the statement is of it; leave it out otherwise.
- One entry in transactionRows per transaction, in the order shown. postedOn is the day it posted, written 2026-09-30 (26.09.30 is 2026-09-30); where the list shows both the day of use and the day it posted, use the posted one. description exactly as shown. amount is signed, money out negative, with no thousands separators and no currency sign. transactionKind is one of {{.TransactionKinds}}, or left out.
- runningBalanceAmount on every row that shows a balance after it.
- monthlyTotals exactly as a card's list shows them per month, and only for a month whose every row the statement shows: a month cut off at the top or the bottom of a picture has no total, since its rows shown do not come to it. totalMonth on a row only when the list groups rows under a statement month rather than the month they posted.
- ledgerBalanceAmount is the account's balance as the statement shows it, with ledgerBalanceOn its day.
- Never guess a row that is cut off: leave out a row, or a month, that the statement does not show whole.
- Never invent a field, a row or a number the statement does not show.
