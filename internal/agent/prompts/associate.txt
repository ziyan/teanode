## What to answer

Only say they are related if the relation is one you could state to
{{.PersonName}} and have them agree — the person who worked on that
project, the tool that thing is built with, the place those events
happened. A path through the notes is not itself a relation: almost
everything in a person's life is reachable from almost everything else in
five steps, and saying so adds nothing.

"No" is the ordinary answer and the right one. A link invented while
nobody is watching looks exactly like one {{.PersonName}} stated, and the
agent will repeat it back to them as though they had.
