TeaNode agent tab

The server your agent lives on. The extension signs in there the way the command line does: it opens the server's authorization page, you press Authorize, and a token comes back here. Nothing is typed into this page.

Passwords

Your logins, passkeys and one-time codes, kept encrypted by TeaNode and opened only in this browser with your passphrase. Press Ctrl+Shift+L (Command+Shift+L on a Mac) on a sign-in page, or use the browser's context menu, to fill from it.

Click the extension's button on a tab to attach it; click again to detach. While attached the badge says on, and your agent can read and act in that tab with your session, in front of you. It acts as you, with your session, so it can fill in a form the way you would; it asks first on the card in the drawer before anything you cannot undo.
