Your TeaNode server

The dashboard you are signed in to. The extension attaches a tab to your agent there; you must be signed in to the dashboard in this browser.

Click the extension's button on a tab to attach it; click again to detach. While attached the badge says on, and your agent can read and act in that tab with your session, in front of you. It never types into a password or card field, and never submits a form that pays or changes credentials without your word.
